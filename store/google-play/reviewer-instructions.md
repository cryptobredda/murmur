# Reviewer access and test route

Murmur has no account/login or paywall. Use an ARM64 Android phone, preferably with 8 GB+ RAM and at least 1.5 GB free storage for one speech model. The free Parakeet download is approximately 670 MB and requires an Internet connection once; local dictation then works offline.

1. Complete onboarding, choose Parakeet TDT v3 and download it in the app. Smart cleanup needs no writing-model download or provider credentials.
2. Grant microphone access and test in-app dictation. Review the resulting text in History and play/reprocess its saved audio.
3. To test cross-app insertion, read and accept the Accessibility disclosure, then enable Murmur in Android Accessibility Settings. Focus a non-password text field in a compatible app, tap the floating control, speak, and tap Finish. The result is inserted or copied when insertion is unavailable.
4. Move the control, change transparency in Settings and try its ten-minute pause target. The microphone is inactive between dictations.
5. Settings offers Accurate/Fast recognition and British/original English spelling. Personal dictionary terms guide Accurate Parakeet recognition. Editing a recent dictation can teach short corrections when learning is enabled.
6. Cloud speech/writing and sync are optional. Local-mode review needs no API keys or external account. Local AI writing and English readback are separately downloaded optional models.

Known platform limits: protected windows/editors can restrict Accessibility insertion or overlays. An Android active-session notification is required during background microphone capture/processing. Accuracy and time vary with speech, noise, model, device and heat.
