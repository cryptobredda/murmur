# Deferred iOS assets

Murmur's iOS app is deferred at the maintainer's request. These are reusable brand assets and marked copy drafts for a future iOS release, not evidence of a working iPhone app.

- `AppIcon-1024.png`: opaque square icon; the OS applies its own corner mask.
- `brand-card-1320x2868.png`: platform-neutral brand artwork, clearly marked as an iOS concept. This is not an App Store app screenshot.
- Listing copy is generated from `store/listing.json` and begins with a submission warning.

Do not upload Android UI captures as iPhone screenshots. Capture the completed iOS app at an accepted current size, prepare its privacy labels and runtime/model licensing, and validate microphone, in-app dictation, History and clipboard workflows before submission. Android-style cross-app overlays are not an iOS capability promised by these assets.
